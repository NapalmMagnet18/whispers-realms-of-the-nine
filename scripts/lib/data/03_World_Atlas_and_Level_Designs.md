World travel network

The six origins travel to Lantern’s Reach. The story runs through Emberstone Quarry, Hollowcrypt and Sorrowfen. At the fork, Tideglass Coast and Deepgrove are separate first-choice stories, only one required; both connect to Velthraen. Then follow Bastion, Glassmere and the Ninth Veil.
Navigation laws
Every zone has one clear entry point, one clear exit point, and at least one alternate route or shortcut through a safe area.
Every dangerous site has visible pre-encounter staging and a return/rescue route.
Map coordinates and POI IDs are schematic; bind them to actual scene transforms only after the Spawn repository is inspected.
Settlement zones support a safe hub, bank, merchant and repair station; high-risk instances must have story-mode alternatives.

World streaming / performance guide
Prioritize small deterministic zones in Spawn; do not build a full seamless world until scene streaming or portals are verified. Keep props batched where supported, prefer authored trigger volumes over costly per-frame scans, and measure party visibility, draw calls and load time for every zone. Regions 1–5 are MVP; further zones are milestones.
Map acceptance checklist
1. Path from Entry to Exit navigates without teleporting into invalid terrain. 2. No dead-end blocks mandatory progress. 3. Quest-giver / turn-in POI always reachable. 4. Respawn point is outside enemy detection. 5. Every important route has distinguishable landmark silhouettes. 6. Solo and multiplayer transitions preserve quest flags. 7. Navigation names match JSON POI strings.

# THE WAYFARER’S COMPANION — FULL PLAYER GAME GUIDE
**WHISPERS: Realms of the Nine | Shattered Accord | Proposed 1–60 Campaign Guide**
**Design-stage notice:** This guide describes **planned gameplay**, not systems confirmed live in Spawn. Level ranges, items, numbers and boss mechanics are targets to balance during playtesting; where a named mechanic is not implemented, treat it as a roadmap spec.
## What game are you playing?
A third-person cooperative MMORPG-style dark fantasy adventure where the central conflict is the right of people and communities to control their own memories. Explore, gather, craft, fight, investigate, and carry evidence between six cultures. The first major story ends at level 60 after the people of the realms form a new witness charter.
## Quick start: the first forty minutes
1. Choose a people and one combat class. Finish all eight origin quests in the protected starting zone (approximate levels 1–5). 2. Travel to Lantern’s Reach and find Gatekeeper Elric at the East Gate. 3. Accept Q001; follow the next seven golden quests to learn combat, foraging, mining, fishing, crafting and archive dialogue. 4. Accept your first class mentor trial after Q001. 5. Repair equipment and bind return to the safe hub. 6. The first regional story begins at the quarry; do not try to enter Hollowcrypt until the quest log marks the entry objective available.

## Six racial starting routes: walkthrough
### Marchfolk: Lantern Ward
Spawn into the protected safe district and talk to Gatekeeper Elric. **Mystery:** A vanished address exposes an erased neighborhood. Complete these eight quests in order. If combat progress feels too hard, do safe gathering steps first and check repair/health supplies.
- `MAR-01` **The Bell Before Dawn** — Speak to Gatekeeper Elric; examine the town census board. Turn in to Gatekeeper Elric.
- `MAR-02` **A Face in the Crowd** — Deliver three supply parcels; ask two neighbors about the vanished address. Turn in to Quartermaster Dren.
- `MAR-03` **The Empty Door** — Follow chalk marks to a walled door; inspect its latch. Turn in to Bram Alder.
- `MAR-04` **Three Names Too Few** — Collect three lost notices; compare them with the bell roll. Turn in to Sister Vale.
- `MAR-05` **Keep the Light** — Light two storm lamps; defend the bell rope from two echo shades. Turn in to Gatekeeper Elric.
- `MAR-06` **A Ledger of Ash** — Collect soot from the old furnace; uncover a brass maker mark. Turn in to Mira the Forgekeeper.
- `MAR-07` **No Grave, No Record** — Read a forgotten memorial plaque; present it to Elric. Turn in to Sister Vale.
- `MAR-08` **Open the Eastern Gate** — Choose whether to archive or publicly display the memorial; travel to Lantern's Reach square. Turn in to Gatekeeper Elric.
After `MAR-08`, follow the marked exit to Lantern’s Reach. The guild’s global finale does not require playing the other origin campaigns.

## Classes and beginner combat

Complete your class chain after Q001; use side quests to practice without replacing shared story.
- `VAN-01` The Weight of a Promise: Equip a shield; intercept two practice strikes aimed at trainees.
- `VAN-02` The Door and the Refugee: Escort three evacuees; choose a defensive position under pressure.
- `VAN-03` The Captain Who Counted: Find a battlefield register; return it to Oren Flint.
- `VAN-04` Stand Where It Hurts: Defend the bridge against three waves; use parry to block the cleave.
- `VAN-05` The Order to Withdraw: Hear two opposing witnesses; decide whether to publish the captain's unlawful order.
- `VAN-06` Names Behind the Rampart: Collect three shield-plate names from the ruins; restore the memorial.
- `VAN-07` The Open Shield: Complete a guardian trial; vow never to use protection to silence the protected.

## Regional walkthrough — main story
### Shared Tutorial: First Light
**Levels:** 5–12 · **Area:** Lantern's Reach / Lantern March. Finish in ascending quest-ID sequence. Keep personal items; copy collected evidence into your quest journal before leaving.
- **Q001 · Welcome to Lantern's Reach:** Talk to Gatekeeper Elric; register your arrival with the Beacon Guild. Why: Elric recognizes the mark carried by every origin, even those he has never seen.
- **Q002 · A Light Against the Briars:** Defeat three wolves; return to Tamsin. Why: The wolves chase the pulse of a hidden bellglass device and need not be exterminated beyond the quest.
- **Q003 · The First Forge:** Gather two ore; smelt one bar; craft a practice blade. Why: The forge rings with a voice saying “There should be nine.”
- **Q004 · The Old River:** Catch two river fish; cook one portion; deliver a meal to the dock watch. Why: A ferry manifest records passage to a dock no citizen can see.
- **Q005 · Supply the Watch:** Deliver three timber bundles; help repair the eastern watch steps. Why: The repaired structure reveals a buried waymark pointing to Hollowcrypt.
- **Q006 · Echoes at the Quarry:** Defeat three quarry scavengers; investigate the damaged bellglass lamp. Why: A fresh Hollow Court maker seal has been pressed over a much older Accord mark.
- **Q007 · Broken Sigil:** Locate three ancient sigil stones; place them in the witness recess. Why: Each stone answers only to a different voice; no solitary hero can restore the inscription.
- **Q008 · The Hollowcrypt Gate:** Inspect the Hollowcrypt seal; invite a party or select story-mode projection; speak with Sister Vale. Why: The first sealed district lies somewhere beneath the town, and the gate accepts witnesses, not owners.
**After completion:** return to the story marker for the next chapter; dungeon story mode is available as a design fallback if multiplayer queues are not implemented.
### Emberstone Quarry: The Price of Stone
**Levels:** 12–17 · **Area:** Emberstone Quarry. Finish in ascending quest-ID sequence. Keep personal items; copy collected evidence into your quest journal before leaving.
- **EQU-01 · A Vein Out of Season:** Sample three new crystal veins; inspect a damaged survey stake. Why: Ore is forming around conversations that were never spoken.
- **EQU-02 · The Quarry Ledger:** Recover two shift ledgers; confront the foreman. Why: Six miners were listed as tools instead of people.
- **EQU-03 · The Machine Beneath:** Activate three safe lift brakes; enter the sealed lower gallery. Why: Someone restarted an Accord machine long after its authorized keepers died.
- **EQU-04 · Ore and Oaths:** Smelt a bellglass-alloy test sample; compare the signal against true copper. Why: Unwitnessed testimony crystallizes under pressure.
- **EQU-05 · Sparks for Sale:** Track a Hollow Court buyer; recover two purchase orders. Why: Court agents are buying bellglass for a device meant to retrieve the erased.
- **EQU-06 · The Man on the Other Side:** Speak across a sealed wall; triangulate the voice with two tuning hammers. Why: The imprisoned miner may be an echo who remembers a real injustice.
- **EQU-07 · Break the Drill:** Defeat the illegal drill crew; stop the unstable rig without harming workers. Why: Mareth's people were promised that excavation would restore loved ones.
- **EQU-08 · Written in the Dust:** Display the miners' names on the quarry board; read the hidden exit coordinates. Why: All six disappeared on the night of the First Silence.
- **EQU-09 · A Road Below the Town:** Open the east shaft connector; report the lost corridor. Why: The route leads beneath the memorial district toward the Hollowcrypt.
**After completion:** return to the story marker for the next chapter; dungeon story mode is available as a design fallback if multiplayer queues are not implemented.
### Sorrowfen: The Unfinished Mourning
**Levels:** 21–25 · **Area:** Sorrowfen. Finish in ascending quest-ID sequence. Keep personal items; copy collected evidence into your quest journal before leaving.
- **SOR-01 · Boardwalk at Dusk:** Activate four ward lanterns; mark a safe evacuation line. Why: The lanterns carry living names stitched into the wicks.
- **SOR-02 · Not All Wisps Are Lost:** Identify three harmless wisps; defeat two coercive tether-spirits. Why: The aggressive spirits are being pulled toward a Court receiver.
- **SOR-03 · Mourner in the Reeds:** Interview a mourner; follow her reflected footsteps. Why: Her missing family is heard only in the reflection, not in the water itself.
- **SOR-04 · The Bell in the Bog:** Recover two sunken bellglass chimes; turn them face-down. Why: The chimes were meant to guide rather than imprison echoes.

## Dungeon and major enemy guide
All encounter mechanics listed below are **planned**, not guaranteed in Spawn until implemented and tested. Accessibility: readable telegraphs, subtitle cues, reduced-flash option, interactable objectives that do not require audio alone, and optional NPC story mode.
### The Candle Warden — Hollowcrypt
**Preparation:** Repair gear, carry healing supplies, designate a safe exit, ensure group members have required entry flags. **Plan:** First telegraph: clockwise lantern swing; stand outside its arc. During the candle phase, light safe urns in the displayed order while avoiding diagonal ground cracks. Read recovered names rather than treating the Warden as a simple monster. **Victory narrative:** Ward light and reclaim the register; no forced kill for story completion.
### Choir Below the Tide — Drowned Bell
**Preparation:** Repair gear, carry healing supplies, designate a safe exit, ensure group members have required entry flags. **Plan:** Watch the water-line rise; move to dry platforms and retune three channel bells. Avoid false echoes that repeat commands. Offer the trapped passengers a choice. **Victory narrative:** Restore one passage without binding the ghost fleet.
### Orchard Heart — Root Archive
**Preparation:** Repair gear, carry healing supplies, designate a safe exit, ensure group members have required entry flags. **Plan:** Dodge root bursts, cut corrupted bellglass tendrils but preserve living roots. Defend the young tree while three separate memory seals are carried to its trunk. **Victory narrative:** Save the grove from harvest without burning its testimony.
### The Bellglass Heart — Bell Foundry
**Preparation:** Repair gear, carry healing supplies, designate a safe exit, ensure group members have required entry flags. **Plan:** Heat zones cycle from red to safe gray; route pressure through old furnaces and disable siphons in order. Evacuate workers before interacting with control ring. **Victory narrative:** Move the power grid away from captive memories.
### Reflections of Ossa — Mirror Hall
**Preparation:** Repair gear, carry healing supplies, designate a safe exit, ensure group members have required entry flags. **Plan:** Illusory quest markers show tempting shortcuts; inspect records with two witnesses, rotate mirror panels without exposing private diaries, use the real withdrawal clause as encounter item. **Victory narrative:** Public records gain a privacy appeal process.
### The Ninth Assembly — Ninth Veil
**Preparation:** Repair gear, carry healing supplies, designate a safe exit, ensure group members have required entry flags. **Plan:** Final encounter is evidence-based, with combat protecting NPC witnesses from unstable echoes. Choose Open Witnessing or Guarded Stewardship after all testimony; both reject forced erasure. **Victory narrative:** Offer Eda the choice to speak, and leave the ninth seat open.

## Crafting, skills and economy
**Core professions:** Mining, Woodcutting, Fishing, Smithing, Alchemy; secondary planned skills include Cooking, Cartography, Trading and Building. These skills create useful interactions, alternate quest XP and an independent economy rather than being mandatory gates to the ending.
### Resource loop
1. Discover a legal node and its region-specific safety marker. 2. Gather with an appropriate tool; node stock and drop limits must be server-authoritative. 3. Process at public stations; retain material provenance. 4. Craft equippable upgrades or consumables. 5. Sell to vendors only at balanced sink/source ratios until secure player-to-player trading is validated.
### Suggested starter recipes
- Iron Shortsword: ordinary iron ingots ×2, timber ×1, forge fuel ×1; grants beginner attack role.
- Traveling Rations: freshwater fish ×1, safe herbs ×1, cooking heat ×1; restores health slowly outside combat.
- Simple Healing Draught: clean flask ×1, moonpetal ×2, water ×1; modest cooldown.
- Wayfarer Lantern: copper ×1, glass ×1, wick ×1; improves navigation in dark routes.
- River Bridge Toolkit: timber ×4, stone ×2, rope ×2; quest construction item.
**No real-money economy is part of the campaign design.** Trading, auction boards and secure cross-player inventories are not assumed already supported in Spawn.
## Itemization and gear progression
Levels 1–10: repaired starter clothing, tools and plain weapons. 11–20: copper/iron field gear. 21–30: swamp/coastal survival set with mobility and control choices. 31–40: observatory and courier sets emphasizing utility. 41–50: Bastion craft gear and warding. 51–60: Witness Set cosmetics awarded for evidence work; no pay-to-win requirements.
## Difficulty and accessibility recommendations
Enemy telegraphs must be visually readable, with optional sound cues rather than sound-only punishments. Keep at least one route back to a safe point. The main story is completable solo in projection mode once implemented, while cooperative dungeons offer social/challenge play. Class-locked infiltration must always have a public evidence alternative.
## Factions, roleplay and social play
The Beacon Guild operates markets and routes; Veilbound preserve archives; Hollow Court houses desperate citizens but has coercive leadership; Shield Table protects evacuation; Wayfarers’ Lodge maps roads; Unseen Door conducts ethical investigation. Local councils preserve cultural difference. Faction reputation unlocks cosmetics, dialogue and auxiliary routes—never requires selecting the same faction as a friend.

## Troubleshooting / player-support checklist
If a quest does not appear, check all listed required IDs and whether the OR gate can be satisfied via another origin/branch. Your character needs to finish one origin, not all six. If an encounter fails after reconnecting, the server should restore objective counters and safe position. A missing party must not permanently block the story: enter story mode or seek the NPC-led projection when supported. Contact support with quest ID and zone POI, not account secrets.
## Post-campaign activities
Return to Lantern’s Reach to witness the public memorial; finish the alternate Tideglass/Deepgrove route; clear optional community and profession chains; learn other peoples’ accounts via testimonies; unlock remaining class-specific cosmetics through alts; explore map shortcuts and documentation clues. The Wanderer and the Cult of Whispers are teased for later books and are not a Season-One boss progression.
## Collectibles & achievements (proposed)
- Nine Bell Listener: complete the main campaign. - All Roads Recorded: finish one origin plus all six testimony vignette collections. - Safe Hands: resolve a Briarwolf snare without harming a cub. - Public Truth: obtain the withdrawal clause. - Open Road: activate all major safe-bridge checkpoints. - Shelter Keeper: complete six local aid quests. - A Name Is Not a Number: finish the Reedbound privacy quest.

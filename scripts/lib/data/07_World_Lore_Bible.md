# WHISPERS: World and Lore Bible (creator-supplied research, 2026-10-08)
Canon reference for quests, NPC lines and zone dressing. Key rules:
- Echo Tide: suppressed memory resurfaces as wrong places, displaced voices, distressed wildlife, resonance.
- The Nine = nine MORTAL Accord keepers (Flame, Root, Stone, Tide, Wind, Mirror, Threshold, Lantern, Silence), not gods; the Nine Old Gods stay mysterious.
- Eras: Before the Silence; Accord of Nine; First Silence (withdrawal clause redacted, a district erased); Lantern Peace (Beacon Guild swaps lamps for bellglass); The Shattered Accord (season one).
- Factions: Beacon Guild (reformed, not destroyed), Veilbound, Hollow Court (Regent Mareth Sable), Shield Table, Wayfarers' Lodge, Unseen Door, local councils, Cult of Whispers (distinct from the Hollow Court).
- Origins: Lantern Ward (Marchfolk), Rootwake Glade (Thren), Emberstone Cradle (Kharic), Breakwater Strand (Namar), Reedhaven Refuge (Reedbound), Starfall Eyrie (Veylori).
- Route: Lantern's Reach 5-12 > Quarry 12-17 > Hollowcrypt 17-21 > Sorrowfen 21-25 > Tideglass OR Deepgrove 24-30 > Velthraen 31-38 > Emberstone Bastion 38-45 > Glassmere 45-52 > Ninth Veil 52-60.
- Atmospheres: Reach brass/slate/amber + bells; Quarry & Bastion red-brown cliffs, copper, soot; Sorrowfen black water, pale lanterns, non-hostile wisps; Tideglass inland lighthouses; Velthraen windharps, silver roofs; Glassmere mirrored water, blank books; Ninth Veil floating ruins of old choices.
- Moral canon: choices are personal flags, never destroy another player's content; finale = Open Witnessing or Guarded Stewardship; Eda keeps her agency; Mareth accountable, not absolved.
- No Warcraft names, races, factions or art.

name: wow-camera
description: Install and wire the World of Warcraft Camera Controls in this world — camera rig, mouse steering and autorun.
---

Load the `wow-camera-controls#wow-camera-controls` skill and follow its install checklist in this world: make the mod's rig the only camera behavior, lock the look while this game's menus are open via `wowCameraLocked`, and wire `lib/motion.js` into the player's controller so right-drag steers, both buttons run and Q autoruns. Then walk it once and judge it by the skill's "Judge it" section.
